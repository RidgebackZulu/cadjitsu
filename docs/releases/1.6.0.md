Cadjitsu 1.6 adds **dark mode**: one click on the moon button and the whole app goes dark, the canvas included, easy on the eyes for long modelling sessions. Light stays the default, exactly as before.

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Cadjitsu-1.6.0-macOS.dmg` | macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later on Apple Silicon |
| `Cadjitsu-1.6.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | 64-bit Linux with X11 and OpenGL |

**macOS:**
1. Open the dmg and drag Cadjitsu onto Applications.
2. The app is ad-hoc signed, so clear the quarantine flag once:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cadjitsu.app`
3. Open Cadjitsu.

**Linux:**
1. Unpack it: `tar xzf Cadjitsu-1.6.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz`
2. Run `Cadjitsu-1.6.0-Linux-x86_64/cadjitsu`.

It brings its own Qt and OpenCASCADE; the system only provides X11 and OpenGL. On Debian or Ubuntu, if something is missing: `sudo apt install libgl1 libegl1 libxkbcommon-x11-0 libxcb-cursor0`.

## What's new in 1.6

**Dark mode**
- **One button.** The moon at the top right of the ribbon, next to MCP, turns everything dark at once; it becomes a sun that turns it back. Cadjitsu remembers the choice next time it opens. Also **View > Dark Mode**.
- **Match system.** **Settings > Appearance > Theme**: Light, Dark, or Match system, which follows the computer's light / dark appearance, and switches with it.
- **Everything goes dark:** the ribbon and its menus, the browser, the timeline, command panels and their guides, dialogs, the sketch palette, value boxes, the navigation bar, and the canvas background and grid.
- **Colours that mean something stay:** the selection blue, Align's orange and blue, errors and warnings, the axes, the "computing" yellow and the materials keep their colours.
- **Still readable:** icon outlines, fixed sketch lines, dimensions and their labels, guide arrows, and the rims of check boxes and radio buttons turn light so they show on the dark panels. The ViewCube stays light, as in Fusion 360.
