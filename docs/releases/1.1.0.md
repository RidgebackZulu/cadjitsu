Cadjitsu 1.1 shows what a part will look like once printed. Each body gets a filament, finish and colour; it stands on a Snapmaker U1-style PEI build plate; a live physically based preview and a path tracer render it.

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Cadjitsu-1.1.0-macOS.dmg` | macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later on Apple Silicon |
| `Cadjitsu-1.1.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | 64-bit Linux with X11 and OpenGL |

**macOS:**
1. Open the dmg and drag Cadjitsu onto Applications.
2. The app is ad-hoc signed, so clear the quarantine flag once:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cadjitsu.app`
3. Open Cadjitsu.

**Linux:**
1. Unpack it: `tar xzf Cadjitsu-1.1.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz`
2. Run `Cadjitsu-1.1.0-Linux-x86_64/cadjitsu`.

It brings its own Qt and OpenCASCADE; the system only provides X11 and OpenGL. On Debian or Ubuntu, if something is missing: `sudo apt install libgl1 libegl1 libxkbcommon-x11-0 libxcb-cursor0`.

## What's new in 1.1

**Print materials per body**
- **Filament:** PLA, PETG or TPU.
- **Finish:** matte, silk or semitransparent.
- **Colour:** named filament colours (Signal Red, Silk Gold, Ice Blue, ...) or any colour. New bodies are grey matte PLA.
- **Browser:** each body shows its colour as a dot on its icon. "Material..." on a body opens the Render dialog for it.
- **Undo:** material changes are undo steps, and they are saved with the design.

**Lifelike rendering**
- **Build plate:** a double-sided PEI spring-steel sheet like the Snapmaker U1's (276 x 293 mm, 270 x 270 mm print area, grab tab), on a black heated bed.
  - Textured PEI, with its golden powder-coat grain, or smooth PEI, which mirrors the model.
  - The model is centred on it, or placed as in a slicer.
- **The print's surface:**
  - layer lines on the walls;
  - staircase steps on slopes;
  - top-skin lines at +45 / -45 degrees;
  - the plate's texture copied onto bottom faces.
- **Materials:**
  - matte filaments are rough and low-gloss;
  - silk filaments have tinted highlights that stretch across the layers;
  - semitransparent prints are hazy and show what's behind them, according to the walls and infill light crosses.
- **Live preview (the Rendered style):**
  - studio or daylight lighting;
  - soft shadows that sharpen near the part, tinted by semitransparent parts;
  - contact shadows and reflections.
- **Ray tracing:**
  - when the view rests, a path tracer takes over the canvas and refines the picture, denoised with Intel Open Image Denoise;
  - light bounces between parts and through semitransparent plastic, with true shadows and reflections;
  - moving the view goes back to the live preview at once.
- **Render dialog** (SOLID > INSPECT > Render): menus for bodies, filament, finish, colour, layer height and lines, infill, build plate and placement, lighting, preview or ray traced, and draft or final quality. Every change shows at once. **Save Image** writes a path-traced picture up to 3840 x 2160.

**AI agents (MCP)**
- `set_material` sets what bodies are printed in, several bodies in one undo step.
- `set_render` sets the plate, lighting, layer height and infill.
- `render_image` saves a path-traced picture of the view.
- `get_design` reports each body's material and the render settings.
- The skill advises on choosing filaments and finishes.

## Notes
- The downloads are larger, because they carry the ray tracer (Embree) and the denoiser.
- The plate's colours are an approximation of the U1's sheet and may be refined.
