Cadjitsu 1.4 makes threads you can print and use: pick any hole or post, choose a size, and get a real helical thread that runs right through, fitted to that size on its own axis, with a countersink for the screw to start on. It adds the extra-fine UNEF series (1/2-28 and friends) and a new front page with screenshots.

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Cadjitsu-1.4.0-macOS.dmg` | macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later on Apple Silicon |
| `Cadjitsu-1.4.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | 64-bit Linux with X11 and OpenGL |

**macOS:**
1. Open the dmg and drag Cadjitsu onto Applications.
2. The app is ad-hoc signed, so clear the quarantine flag once:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cadjitsu.app`
3. Open Cadjitsu.

**Linux:**
1. Unpack it: `tar xzf Cadjitsu-1.4.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz`
2. Run `Cadjitsu-1.4.0-Linux-x86_64/cadjitsu`.

It brings its own Qt and OpenCASCADE; the system only provides X11 and OpenGL. On Debian or Ubuntu, if something is missing: `sudo apt install libgl1 libegl1 libxkbcommon-x11-0 libxcb-cursor0`.

## What's new in 1.4

**Threads that print and fit**
- **Any hole or post, any size.** Pick a hole's wall or a post and a size: the cylinder is first fitted to the thread on its own axis. A hole too big is filled and bored again, one too small opened; a post too thin is grown out, one too fat turned down. The panel says what will happen ("A 12.70 mm hole, filled and bored to 1/2-28 UNEF").
- **Always modelled.** Every thread is a real helical ISO profile with print clearance (0.15 mm by default). It runs right out through open faces; at a blind bottom or a shoulder it fades out to the root. A post's tip gets a 45° chamfer.
- **Countersinks.** A threaded hole gets a countersink so a screw starts true: on the **Top** (the face drilled into, by default), the **Bottom** or **Both**, with the included **Angle** (90° metric, 82° inch, 100°, 120°) and the **Diameter** at the face (blank: a little over the thread). The same options are on Hole > Tapped.
- **Recent sizes first.** The five thread sizes you used last head the size lists, in the Thread tool and in Hole > Tapped.
- **Never held up.** A long thread is computed in the background and abandoned the moment you pick something else; while a preview takes a while, the panel says *Computing the preview…* and the hole lights up in yellow.

**More sizes**
- UNEF extra fine, #12 to 7/8" (1/2-28, 3/4-20…), and the in-between UNC and UNF sizes (#2, #5, #12, 9/16", 7/8").

**AI agents (MCP)**
- `thread`: `countersink`, `countersink_ends`, `countersink_angle`, `countersink_diameter`; any hole or post takes any `size`.
- Tapped `hole`: `thread_countersink`, `thread_countersink_ends`, `thread_countersink_angle`, `thread_countersink_diameter`.

**Front page**
- The README is now a landing page with pictures of what Cadjitsu makes, all built through the MCP tools by the `showcase` self test (`scripts/readme_images.sh` renders them again).

**Changed**
- The **Thread Type** choice is gone: threads are always modelled (`mode` and `thread_mode` are ignored over MCP). Designs that left small threads as a tap drill now get the thread.
- Countersinks go on the top end by default, not both ends.
