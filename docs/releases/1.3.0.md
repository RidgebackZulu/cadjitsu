Cadjitsu 1.3 turns photos into parts: put a photo on a plane, correct its lens and perspective, and trace the part into a sketch at true size. It also brings Revolve and Shell, and the everyday sketch tools Trim, Extend, Fillet, Slot and Polygon. AI agents can now do all of it too: they see the photo with a millimetre grid, trace it and add your caliper measurements.

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Cadjitsu-1.3.0-macOS.dmg` | macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later on Apple Silicon |
| `Cadjitsu-1.3.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | 64-bit Linux with X11 and OpenGL |

**macOS:**
1. Open the dmg and drag Cadjitsu onto Applications.
2. The app is ad-hoc signed, so clear the quarantine flag once:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cadjitsu.app`
3. Open Cadjitsu.

**Linux:**
1. Unpack it: `tar xzf Cadjitsu-1.3.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz`
2. Run `Cadjitsu-1.3.0-Linux-x86_64/cadjitsu`.

It brings its own Qt and OpenCASCADE; the system only provides X11 and OpenGL. On Debian or Ubuntu, if something is missing: `sudo apt install libgl1 libegl1 libxkbcommon-x11-0 libxcb-cursor0`.

## What's new in 1.3

**Reverse engineering from photos**
- **Canvas** (SOLID > INSERT, or File): a picture on an origin plane, construction plane or planar face, see-through under your sketch. It is saved in the design, sits in the browser's Canvases folder, and every change is one undo step.
- **Calibrate**: click two marks a known distance apart (a ruler, a coin, a caliper's jaws) and type the real distance.
- **Correct Lens**: click along a few edges that are straight in reality; the barrel or pincushion bend of a phone or wide-angle lens is measured and taken out. A preview shows the straightened photo.
- **Correct Perspective**: lay the part on a sheet of paper, click the sheet's corners and type its size (A4 by default); the photo becomes a true top view at scale.
- **Trace Canvas** (SKETCH > CREATE): hover the part and its outline previews, fitted with lines, arcs and circles, holes included; click to add it with horizontal, vertical and tangent constraints. Type a sensitivity to take in more or less.
- **Views** (SOLID > INSERT): pictures from the front, the right side and the top, set up on XZ, YZ and XY at one scale from a single measured size, lined up as a projection. It warns when the views disagree.

**Solids**
- **Revolve**: turn profiles about a sketch line, an edge or an axis; one side, symmetric or two sides; new body, join, cut or intersect.
- **Shell**: hollow a body to an even wall, removing the faces you pick (or with a closed void); inside or outside. It warns when walls are thinner than two extrusion lines.

**Sketch**
- **Trim** (T): click or drag across pieces to cut them back to the nearest crossings. Text moves to Shift+T.
- **Extend**: run a line or arc on to the next curve.
- **Fillet**: round a corner with a tangent arc; the lines' dimensions still measure to the sharp corner.
- **Slot** and **Polygon** (inscribed, or across flats for nut traps; Space switches).

**AI agents (MCP)**
- `revolve`, `shell`, `sketch_trim`, `sketch_extend`, `sketch_fillet`, and `slot` / `regular_polygon` sketch entities.
- Photos: `insert_canvas`, `calibrate_canvas`, `canvas_perspective`, `canvas_lens`, `insert_views`.
- `canvas_image` shows the agent the picture with a labelled millimetre grid (or zoomed on a detail), `trace_canvas` traces a part from a point inside it, and `sketch_dimension` sets measured sizes. Sketch curves list their point ids.
