Cadjitsu 1.2 brings Fusion 360-style sketch tools: mirror and circular patterns with live dialogs, construction lines, and lines projected from other sketches that stay linked to them.

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Cadjitsu-1.2.0-macOS.dmg` | macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later on Apple Silicon |
| `Cadjitsu-1.2.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | 64-bit Linux with X11 and OpenGL |

**macOS:**
1. Open the dmg and drag Cadjitsu onto Applications.
2. The app is ad-hoc signed, so clear the quarantine flag once:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cadjitsu.app`
3. Open Cadjitsu.

**Linux:**
1. Unpack it: `tar xzf Cadjitsu-1.2.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz`
2. Run `Cadjitsu-1.2.0-Linux-x86_64/cadjitsu`.

It brings its own Qt and OpenCASCADE; the system only provides X11 and OpenGL. On Debian or Ubuntu, if something is missing: `sudo apt install libgl1 libegl1 libxkbcommon-x11-0 libxcb-cursor0`.

## What's new in 1.2

**Mirror** (SKETCH > MODIFY, or right-click)
- A dialog opens at once: **Objects**, then the **Mirror Line**, a line or a sketch axis, named in the dialog ("Y axis").
- The mirror image shows before you commit, and it stays symmetric to the original when the original changes.
- Points on the mirror line are shared, so half an outline drawn up to the line becomes one closed profile.

**Circular Pattern**
- A dialog: **Objects**, **Centre Point** (a point, the origin or anywhere), **Quantity** (the original included) and **Angle**.
- The copies redraw as you type. Copied dimensions follow the original's.

**Picking in the dialogs**
- Click a field to pick into it; its × clears it.
- Box-select Objects: drag left to right for what lies inside, right to left for what the box touches.

**Construction geometry**
- Press **X**, or right-click a line and choose Make Construction / Make Normal.
- Construction lines are dotted and never make profiles; they are guides for the drawing.

**Project** (P)
- Earlier sketches show faintly; click their lines, circles, arcs or points to bring them into this sketch, usually from another plane.
- Projected geometry is purple and fixed, and follows its original when it changes. It works as a normal line, and can be made construction and back.

**Browser**
- Right-click a sketch for **Edit Sketch** or **Delete**. It asks first if features use the sketch, and one undo brings it back.

**AI agents (MCP)**
- `sketch_mirror`, `sketch_pattern`, `project_to_sketch` and `set_construction`.
- Sketches report their curves with ids, so an agent can pick them.
