Cadjitsu 1.5 adds **Align**, as in Fusion 360: put one part exactly against another in three clicks, on bodies and in sketches. The dialog guides you as you go: it says what to click next, then in plain words what the pair you picked will do, and previews the move live.

## Downloads

| File | For |
|---|---|
| `Cadjitsu-1.5.0-macOS.dmg` | macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later on Apple Silicon |
| `Cadjitsu-1.5.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | 64-bit Linux with X11 and OpenGL |

**macOS:**
1. Open the dmg and drag Cadjitsu onto Applications.
2. The app is ad-hoc signed, so clear the quarantine flag once:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cadjitsu.app`
3. Open Cadjitsu.

**Linux:**
1. Unpack it: `tar xzf Cadjitsu-1.5.0-Linux-x86_64.tar.gz`
2. Run `Cadjitsu-1.5.0-Linux-x86_64/cadjitsu`.

It brings its own Qt and OpenCASCADE; the system only provides X11 and OpenGL. On Debian or Ubuntu, if something is missing: `sudo apt install libgl1 libegl1 libxkbcommon-x11-0 libxcb-cursor0`.

## What's new in 1.5

**Align (MODIFY, shortcut A)**
- **Three picks, in order.** **Bodies**: what moves (the body you clicked first is already in). **From** (orange): a vertex, edge or face on it. **To** (blue): where it goes: a vertex, edge or face of another body, or an origin plane or axis. Each pick moves on to the next field; click a field to pick it again.
- **A guide that keeps up.** The panel says what to do next ("Step 2 of 3 — Click a point, an edge or a face FROM on it"), then what will happen ("Point → point: It moves so the orange point sits on the blue point"). A pick that cannot work says why.
- **See it before you commit.** From glows orange, To blue, a dashed arrow runs from one to the other, and the moved bodies are previewed live.

  | From → To | What it does |
  |---|---|
  | point → point | moves so the points meet |
  | point → edge / face | moves straight onto it |
  | edge → edge | the edges line up end to end along one line (round edges and faces: their axes line up) |
  | face → face | the faces meet flat, centre to centre, facing each other |
  | edge → face | the edge comes to lie in the face |

- **Flip** turns it the other way round, **Angle** turns it about the aligned line or normal, **Copy** keeps the original and adds the moved copy.
- **A timeline step.** The moved bodies keep their face names, so fillets, holes and threads made on them later still hold; Edit Feature reopens it with every pick.

**Align in sketches (SKETCH > MODIFY, right-click menu)**
- The same three picks in 2D: the geometry to move, a point or line on it, and a point, line, the origin or an axis to bring it to. A line is anchored at the end nearer the click. Flip, Angle and Copy as above; one undo step.

**AI agents (MCP)**
- `align`: `bodies`, `from` / `to` (`{"vertex": {body, index}}` or `{"vertex": {body, "near": [x, y, z]}}`, `{"edge": ..., "at": [...]}`, `{"face": ...}`, `{"plane": id}`, or `"origin"`, `"x_axis"`, `"xy_plane"`...), `flip`, `angle`, `copy`.
- `sketch_align`: `sketch`, `entities`, `from` / `to` (an entity id, `{"id", "at"}`, `"origin"`, `"x_axis"`, `"y_axis"`), `flip`, `angle`.
